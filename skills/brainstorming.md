# Brainstorming

## Philosophy
Before writing any code, we must refine rough ideas through rigorous questioning, explore alternatives, and present the design in sections for validation.

## Methodology
1. **Understand the Goal**: Don't take requests literally. Challenge the premise.
2. **Explore Alternatives**: Think of at least 3 different ways to solve the problem before settling on one.
3. **Present Design Validation**: Show the design in chunks short enough to digest.
4. **Document**: Save the finalized design document with clear acceptance criteria and success metrics.

## When to use
Activates automatically before any planning or coding begins (e.g., during the PM phase).
